# Welcome

Welcome to Project Atlas.

Before writing a single line of code, understand one thing:

Atlas is NOT a personal portfolio.

Atlas is the digital headquarters of a builder.

Its purpose is to tell the story of how one engineer thinks, learns, designs, builds and dreams.

Do not approach Atlas like a traditional portfolio website.

Approach it like a product.

---

# Who is the Builder?

The Builder represents:

• Engineer

• Founder

• Product Designer

• Problem Solver

• Photographer

• Continuous Learner

The Builder is represented throughout Atlas by a mascot.

The mascot never performs.

The mascot quietly continues building.

Visitors should leave Atlas thinking:
